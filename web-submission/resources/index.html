What is Artistic?

A multimodal ASD screening system built for clinicians and researchers

Early and reliable screening for autism spectrum disorder remains challenging in low-resource settings. Artistic combines three complementary signal streams with a dedicated explainability component, into a single fused prediction.

Beyond prediction, the platform provides transcript-level behavioral annotations, global and local feature attributions via SHAP, and counterfactual what-if analysis so clinicians understand why a prediction was made.

Component 01
Pragmatic & Conversational

207 features, turn-taking, topic coherence, pause/latency, repair detection.

Component 02
Acoustic & Prosodic

153 features, pitch, MFCC, spectral, energy, and rhythm from child audio segments.

Component 03
Syntactic & Semantic

27 features, POS tagging, dependency parsing, and morphosyntax via spaCy.

Component 04
Explainability Layer

SHAP global and local attributions, plus counterfactual analysis to show what changes influence the final prediction.
