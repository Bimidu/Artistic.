About the Project

Research Context

Institution

Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), undergraduate research in the Faculty of Computing.

Duration

Research conducted throughout the 2025, 2026 academic year, with preliminary clinical validation in 2026.

Research Focus

This project sits at the intersection of natural language processing, audio signal processing, and explainable machine learning, applied to neurodevelopmental disorder screening.

This system is a screening tool, not a clinical diagnostic instrument. IRB approval is pending for clinical pilots. All training data is sourced from TalkBank's publicly available ASDBank corpora.

Artistic

Machine Learning-Based Early Detection of Autism Using Multimodal Conversational Features. A research project at SLIIT.
